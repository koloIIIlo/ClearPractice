# Appendix D: Copy deck and wording rules

These strings are the starting point. Change them freely, but keep the rules.

**Language and format**
- en-GB spelling (D-9).
- Sentence case for headings and buttons.
- Buttons are verbs: Start, Save, Send, Try anyway.
- At most one exclamation mark, and only in a celebration headline.

## D.1 Rules

1. **Praise the task, never the person.**
   - Yes: "Three takes where pauses took the place of most hesitations."
   - No: "You're a natural!", "Perfect!", "You're fluent now!"

   Kluger & DeNisi (1996): feedback aimed at the self can lower performance.
2. **Show where every number came from** (Measured, Estimated, Your note, AI suggestion), and say what it counts. Never call a number a score.
3. **Never guess feelings from a voice.** Self-reports are labelled as yours: "your rating", "you noted".
4. **No pressure language.** Never write "Don't break your streak", "You'll lose", "Last chance", "Hurry" or "You missed". Write "New week, fresh start" instead.
5. **Missing data is normal.** Say what's needed: "Needs local transcription", "Review 5 takes to set your starting point". Never write "Failed" or "0".
6. **Offer, don't push.** Write "Try anyway", "Not now", "Skip". Every suggestion can be dismissed without guilt.

**Banned in UI text.** A content test checks for these words outside the documentation:
- score, scored, grade, graded, rating of your voice, percentage next to a measure;
- perfect, flawless, native-like, fluent (as a verdict);
- fail or failed (for practice), streak lost, punish;
- anxious, nervous, stressed (as a label for you). Allowed only inside the user's own notes, or when quoting research in Help.

## D.2 Style choice (7.5, 9.1)

**Card on Today**
- **Title:** "New: levels, effort points and achievements"
- **Body:** "They reward practising, never how your voice sounds. Prefer it calm? You can switch any time in Settings."
- **Buttons:** "Keep it playful" · "Choose calm"

**Settings → Practice style**
- **Playful:** "Levels, effort points, achievements and short celebrations. Nothing is ever scored."
- **Calm:** "Just practice and evidence. Your progress is still kept, so you can switch back any time."
- **Calm note on the Journey page:** "You're in Calm style. This page isn't in the sidebar."

## D.3 Journey (9.x)

**Page and header**
- **Empty state:** "Your journey starts with your first take. Each area below shows what to try first."
- **XP explainer** ("How XP works"):

  > "You earn XP for practising: 10 for a take, 5 more for each retake, 20 for finishing a session, 30 for a real conversation you note, and so on. XP measures effort only. It never measures how you sound. A typical session earns about 80."

**Levels**
- **How levels work:**

  > "Levels show what you've practised and shown. They are never a grade of your English. AI suggestions don't count toward them. A level you reach is never taken away."
- **Level states:** "Reached 12 Oct" · "Ready: you've already met this" · "In progress" · "Not yet".
- **Unavailable criterion:** "Needs local transcription. [Set it up]" · "Arrives with Drills" · "Review 5 takes to set your starting point".

**Soft lock:** "Recommended after Spontaneity 3 · Try anyway"

**Weekly goal**
- **Progress:** "2 of 3 days this week · resets Monday · rest days are part of practice"
- **Met:** "Weekly goal reached: 3 of 3 days. Rest well."
- **Weeks in a row:** "3 weeks in a row"

**Fresh starts**
- **Monday:** "New week. Three quests are ready."
- **First week of a month:** "October: Storytelling month. Quests lean toward stories."
- **Comeback:** "Welcome back. Today's plan is short; pick up where it feels right."

**Quests**
- **Swap:** "Swap (1 left this week)".
- **Reason, example:** "Voice & delivery has had the least practice this month."

## D.4 Celebrations (9.10)

**General rules**
- **Headline:** at most 40 characters.
- **"Because" line:** at most 90 characters, describing the task.
- **Buttons:** "See in Journey" · "Close".

| Kind | Headline | Because line |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Level | "Fluency · level 2 · Replacing" | "3 reviewed takes with clearly fewer confirmed hesitations than your first five." |
| Level | "Spontaneity · level 3 · No notice" | "3 unfamiliar questions answered with 10 seconds or less to prepare." |
| Weekly goal | "Weekly goal reached" | "3 of 3 days. That's 2 weeks in a row." |
| Achievement | "Achievement: Said it without notes" | "A minute or more with an empty outline." |
| Record (card) | "New personal record" | "Longest run: 14 words (previous 11, 3 Oct)." |
| Record (toast) | "New best: widest vocabulary, 39 in every 50" | — |
| Quest | toast only: "Quest done: Review 10 phrases · +30 XP" | — |
| Companion | toast only: "Sprout grew a new leaf" | — |

**Overflow:** "And 2 more in Journey".

## D.5 Studio and summaries

**Take saved toast (Playful):** "Take 2 saved · +15 XP"

**What went well:** "Your longest run was 14 words, from 'So the first…'" · "Every pause fell between clauses."

**One thing to try**
- "Pause at the end of a thought, not in the middle (2 pauses fell inside a clause)."
- "Try opening with your point."
- "Notice how often you say 'actually' (3 times)."

**Self-checks**
- **Questions:** "Did you open with your point?" · "Did you finish clearly?" · "Did you vary your voice on purpose?"
- **Answers:** "Yes" · "Not this time"

**Live pace:** "About 4.1 syllables a second · Estimated" · "Listening…" · Hint: "There's no right speed; it shows your range."

## D.6 Drills (10.x)

**Just a Minute**
- **Rules:** "Speak for 60 seconds without hesitation, repetition or going off topic. Press F to flag a slip you notice."
- **Result:** "About 47 clean seconds · Estimated" · "Hesitation candidates 3 · Long pauses 1 · Repeats 2 · Your flags 1"
- **Without a transcript:** "Clean seconds need a transcript. Long pauses and your flags are shown."

**Topic roulette lights:** "Green: minimum time reached" · "Amber: nearly time" · "Red: time".

**Word bomb**
- **Live:** "Weave in: umbrella"
- **Result:** "Words woven in: 2 of 3 · Estimated"

**Pace ladder:** "Range, not speed: being able to slow down on purpose is the skill."

**Emphasis game**
- **Result:** "Strongest beat: *say* · Estimated"
- **When the beats don't line up:** "Couldn't line up the beats this time. Listen and judge yourself."

**Daily prompt**
- **Share text:** "ClearPractice Daily #123 · 1:00 / 🟩🟩🟨🟩⬜🟩 / 4 flowing · 1 hesitation · 1 pause"
- **Copied:** "Copied. It has no audio and none of your words."

## D.7 Confidence (12.x)

- **Page intro:** "Speaking nerves are common and they ease with gradual practice. The ratings here are your own reading, never measured from your voice."
- **Discomfort scale:** "How uncomfortable do you expect this to feel? (your rating)" · anchors "None", "Noticeable", "Very strong" · value "6 of 10".
- **Next step:** "This one feels manageable now. Ready for the next step?"
- **Routine end:** "You're ready. Good luck."
- **Worry box:** "Write whatever is on your mind for one minute. It's discarded at the end unless you keep it."

## D.8 Partner (13.x)

**Hub intro:** "Practise conversations. Your turns are recorded and transcribed on this computer; only the text of your answers is sent, and only when you press Send."

**Send bar**
- **API:** "Send your answer to OpenAI · gpt-6-luna · at most $0.0006 · this conversation $0.0021 of $0.05 · [Send]"
- **Local:** "Send to the local model on this computer · free · [Send]"
- **Local, auto-send on:** "Sending to the local model…"

**Messages**
- **Stale preview:** "Your answer changed since the preview. Preview again."
- **Cost cap:** "This conversation reached its cost limit ($0.05). End it, or raise the limit in Partner settings."
- **No voices:** "No offline English voice found on this computer, so the partner's lines show as captions. You can add a voice in Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech."
- **Scripted mode:** "Scripted (offline): the partner asks prepared questions and doesn't adapt to your answers."

## D.9 Listener pack page (`listen.html`, 14.4)

- **Title:** "{Name} asked for your thoughts"
- **Intro:** "Listen to the recording (about {m} min), then answer the questions. Nothing is sent anywhere: save the answer file and send it back to {Name} however you like."
- **Button:** "Save my answers"
- **Done:** "Saved as answers.cpanswer.json. Send it to {Name}. Thank you!" (the one allowed exclamation mark)

## D.10 Errors

Engagement never shows errors to the user. Its failures are only logged in development.

| Situation | Message |
| --- | --- |
| A drill take is interrupted | the recorder's existing messages, plus "This round's take is in 'Your recordings on this computer'." |
| Listener answers come from another pack | "These answers belong to a pack this computer didn't make. Nothing was imported." |
| An answer file is too large or broken | "This answer file couldn't be read. Nothing was imported." |
| A challenge code is wrong | "That code doesn't look right. Check the last two characters." |
