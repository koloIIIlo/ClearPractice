## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-18

### Added

- M7 foundations: a local practice-event history, saved activity settings, shared recording controls and a choice of Playful or Calm practice.
- M8 insights: flow and word observations, self-checks, review markers, phrase-use tracking, personal trends, blind A/B listening and an optional live pace estimate.
- M9 Journey: practice skills, effort rewards, achievements, personal records, weekly quests and goals, celebrations and Sprout. Calm style hides the game layer and keeps the history.
- M10 drills: thirteen short activities, daily challenges, mixed practice and interview rounds.
- M11 learning: prompts to answer again, a challenge dial, warm-up games, guided programmes, optional predictions and Catch it listening practice.
- M12 confidence: a practice ladder, a pre-talk routine, after-action reviews and saved favourite moments.
- M13 partner: scripted offline, cloud and local-model conversations, listener panels, answer upgrades and conversation feedback. Cloud turns keep their preview, Send action and cost cap.
- M14 real world: weekly missions, event plans, talk outlines, cue cards, rehearsals, listener feedback files and monthly time capsules.
- M15 social: Club mode, a separate timing light, challenge codes, buddy feedback and Copy my week.
- M16 platform additions: optional surprise prompts with a separate Windows notification opt-in, optional first-language practice tips, user documentation and What's new. Language tips remain drafts pending speaker review.
- Local recording and transcription, editable transcripts, phrase review, session search, backups and restore, optional provider coaching and session export.

### Changed

- Pages and the command palette load on demand to reduce start-up JavaScript. A bundle check enforces the start-up budget.
- Recording, transcription queues, playback and keyboard navigation share consistent controls across exercises.

### Fixed

- Coaching approvals become invalid when the transcript or hidden words change.
- Unfamiliar-task trends use the first take, keeping rehearsed retries separate.
- Exports include the take's transcript, corrections, observations and saved feedback.
